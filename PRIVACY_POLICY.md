# Privacy Policy for KidShield

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

Welcome to KidShield. We take children's privacy and parental trust very seriously.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
KidShield does not collect, track, or share any personal identity information, phone numbers, contacts, device IDs, or browsing data.

### 2. Permissions & Usage
- **Display Over Other Apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`)**: Required only to display the selected safety lock screen over active apps when the parent-set timer expires.
- **Foreground Service**: Needed to keep the countdown timer running accurately in the background while other apps are active.
- **Post Notifications**: Needed on Android 13+ to show persistent timer countdown status.

### 3. Monetization & Payment
Payments made via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) are processed directly by the respective bank/UPI application on your phone. KidShield does not collect or store card numbers or banking passwords.

### 4. Contact
For any queries, contact: support@kidshield.app
