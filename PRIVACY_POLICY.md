# Privacy Policy for KidShield

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 26, 2026  
**Application:** KidShield - Screen Time & Lock (`com.example.kidshield`)

Welcome to **KidShield**. We are deeply committed to protecting family privacy and child safety. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, permissions, and security.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
KidShield does **NOT** collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, contact books, precise/approximate GPS locations, audio/video recordings, photos, or device hardware identifiers.

### 2. Device Permissions Used & Technical Purpose
KidShield strictly requests only the minimal permissions required for on-device parental control functionality:
- **Display Over Other Apps (`android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`)**:  
  Used solely to display the chosen screen time safety simulation overlay (e.g. Empty Battery, Temperature Warning, or Offline screen) over running apps when the parental timer completes.
- **Foreground Service (`android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE`)**:  
  Required to ensure the countdown timer runs accurately and continuously in the background while other applications are in use.
- **Post Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**:  
  Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display the ongoing countdown timer status in the notification shade.

> **Local Processing:** All timers, overlay triggers, and screen locks are computed **100% locally on the device**. No data is ever transmitted to external servers.

---

### 3. Advertising Policy (100% Ad-Free)
KidShield is **100% Ad-Free**. The application contains **NO third-party advertisements, NO advertising tracking SDKs, and NO behavioral trackers**.

---

### 4. Voluntary Contributions & Payment Information
KidShield offers optional voluntary donations via standard Indian Unified Payments Interface (UPI) applications (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm). 
- All transactions are executed securely through the user's third-party UPI banking app.
- KidShield **never accesses, stores, or handles bank account numbers, debit/credit cards, or UPI PINs**.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
KidShield is designed as a parental utility to assist parents in managing their children's screen time.
- We do not solicit, gather, or process personal data from children under the age of 13.
- If you have any reason to believe personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately for prompt resolution.

---

### 6. Security of Your Information
Because KidShield operates purely locally on your device without user account registration or cloud databases, your data and device usage stay strictly within your physical device.
