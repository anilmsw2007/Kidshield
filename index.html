Privacy Policy for KidShield

Effective Date: November 26, 2026
Application: KidShield Parental Control (com.example.kidshield)

Welcome to KidShield. We prioritize child safety and family privacy above everything else. This Privacy Policy outlines our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

KidShield does NOT collect, track, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, contact lists, GPS location, device identifiers, or browsing activity.

2. Android Permissions Used & Justification

KidShield only requests permissions essential for core parental control functionality:

  • Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely to display the chosen safety simulation screen (e.g. Empty Battery or Temperature Warning) over active apps when the parent-set timer expires.
  • Foreground Service (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE): Required to maintain an uninterrupted countdown timer in the background while children use the phone.
  • Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to display timer countdown progress in the notification shade.

All timer computations, screen locks, and muting events operate 100% locally on your device without network data transmission.

3. COPPA & Global Child Privacy Compliance

KidShield strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not solicit personal details from children under any circumstances.

4. Payments & Monetization

Upgrades to KidShield Pro are conducted via direct Indian UPI applications (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm). KidShield does not collect, process, or store bank credentials, card numbers, or UPI PINs.
